andClaw Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 12, 2026

1. Data Collection

andClaw does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data remains on your device.

2. Locally Stored Data

The following information stays only on your device and is never sent to CoderRed:

  • API Keys – Your AI provider API keys are stored in the app's private storage.
  • Chat Messages – Processed through the on-device gateway and sent directly to your chosen AI provider.
  • Channel Tokens – WhatsApp, Telegram, and Discord bot tokens are stored locally.
  • App Settings – Preferences such as model selection and auto-start settings.

3. Third-Party Services

andClaw communicates directly with your selected AI provider. Your usage is subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • OpenRouter
  • Anthropic
  • OpenAI
  • Google AI

4. Messaging Channels

When you connect messaging channels (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord), messages are processed locally on your device and forwarded to your configured AI provider. CoderRed does not have access to these messages.

5. Children's Privacy

andClaw is not intended for use by children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at coderredlab@gmail.com.
